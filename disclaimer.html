Scientific & Educational Disclaimer

Effective Date: July 24, 2026 | Last Updated: 2026

1. General Educational Information Only

All content, chemical data, physical metrics, electronic configurations, and 3D visual representations published on FRZI Labs (https://preodic-table-explainer.vercel.app/) are provided for general educational, academic reference, and informational purposes only.

Although our chemical element datasets are carefully researched and compiled from peer-reviewed scientific institutions (including IUPAC, NIST, PubChem, and RSC), FRZI Labs makes no warranties, express or implied, regarding the absolute completeness, accuracy, or suitability for industrial laboratory applications of any dataset published on this platform.

2. Verification Through Authoritative Sources

Scientific data and chemical constants can evolve as analytical measurement techniques advance. Users, students, researchers, and engineers should independently verify critical scientific calculations, experimental parameters, thermodynamic metrics, and toxicity safety data through primary academic sources, official Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS), or official IUPAC reports before undertaking laboratory experiments or commercial chemical synthesis.

3. Laboratory Handling & Safety Disclaimer

Descriptions of elemental reactivity, toxicity, radiological properties, or oxidation behavior contained on FRZI Labs do NOT constitute a laboratory safety manual or operational protocol. Chemical elements (such as reactive alkali metals, halogen gases, or heavy radioactive isotopes) possess extreme physical and chemical hazards.

Never attempt physical chemical experiments or reagent handling based solely on information read on this website. Always consult certified chemical hygiene officers, institutional safety guidelines, and official MSDS documents.

4. External Links Disclaimer

FRZI Labs may contain links to external academic websites, institutional databases, or third-party resources. Such external links are provided solely for user convenience. FRZI Labs does not endorse, control, or guarantee the scientific accuracy or safety of content found on external linked sites.

5. Contact for Data Corrections

If you identify an error, typo, or outdated numerical constant in our Periodic Table element entries, we encourage you to inform our team immediately. Please email us at ankitgemini4@gmail.com with the details and citation source so we can review and update the information promptly.
