The Breath of the Cosmos

Oxygen is a highly reactive nonmetal, and an oxidizing agent that readily forms oxides with most elements as well as with other compounds. By mass, oxygen is the third-most abundant element in the universe, after hydrogen and helium. At standard temperature and pressure, two atoms of the element bind to form dioxygen, a colorless and odorless diatomic gas.

Where is it found?

Earth's Crust

Almost half of the Earth's crust is made of oxygen, bound in silicates and other minerals.

The Atmosphere

Diatomic oxygen gas constitutes 20.95% of the Earth's atmosphere by volume, essential for most terrestrial life.

Applications & Uses

monitor_heart

Medicine

Supplemental oxygen therapy is crucial for treating respiratory diseases, trauma, and during surgical procedures.

eco

Nature

The oxygen cycle relies on photosynthesis by plants and phytoplankton, maintaining atmospheric equilibrium.

rocket_launch

Aerospace

Liquid oxygen (LOX) is the most common oxidizer used in liquid propellant rockets to achieve orbit.

Essential Compounds

The most famous oxygen compound is water (H₂O). The unique bent shape of the molecule gives water its polar properties.
